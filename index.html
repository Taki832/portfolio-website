About Me

The person behind the code

I'm Vishnu, a second-year BSc CS student at Mannar Thirumalai Naicker College (Autonomous) in Pasumalai, Madurai. Roll number 24SUCS144.

I build systems, not demos. A trading bot that intercepts WebSocket traffic without any official API. A marketplace with actual concurrency protection. An AI assistant that runs completely offline. If I ship something, it works.

My deeper interest is in the parts of CS that won't get replaced: operating systems, distributed systems, security, and networking. I want to know how computers work, not just how to call APIs.

I also co-authored two conference research papers — one on a deepfake detection framework (Telecom-Shield), and another on AI-based smart energy management for solar cars.

"I prefer brutal honesty and real feedback over being babied. If my code is broken, tell me why — I'll fix it."

Currently exploring freelance work and actively looking for an internship where I can solve real problems, not just make slides about solving them.
